# Changelog

## 1.0.0

The guarantee engine, the kitchen behind it and the bill in front of it, in one
store file.

**The covers.** A guaranteed count with a business day deadline, the house
overset that sets the room above it, special meals coming out of the guarantee
rather than on top, and the course split the room actually ordered.

**The kitchen.** Recipes held as batches with a yield factor, a production
factor per service style, the explosion to plates, batches, ingredients and
packs, requisitions on the stores, the shelf, purchase orders netted against it,
plate costing from the case price up, and what actually went out on the night.

**The money.** Effective dated prices and labour rates, charge lines, service
charge and tax by category, invoices, deposits, food and beverage minimums,
cancellation damages on a published scale, planner commission on any of the
three bases anybody argues about, statements aged from the terms the account was
given, and the service charge handed back to the crew by hours times points.

**The building.** Rooms, airwalls and combinations, the diary, tentative holds
ranked first and second, room turns between two setups, and the store cupboard
against what two overlapping functions both want.

**The paper.** Banquet event orders as frozen revisions with drift detection and
change orders, a distribution list and who acknowledged what, the run of show,
the production and pick sheets, the day sheet, the pace report, and a Monday
sweep for everything that quietly stopped adding up.

Everything is a command, everything takes `--format json` or `csv`, and the read
service serves the same answers over HTTP with entity tags on them.
